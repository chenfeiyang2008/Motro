# Admin Enrichment Review

**Responsibility:** make an accountable human decision on one enrichment draft at a time.

**Primary action:** `接受并下一条`; `驳回` and `保存修改` are secondary and explicit.

**Required content:** English spelling, Wiktionary facts and provenance, DeepSeek Chinese draft visibly labeled as AI draft, editable accepted fields, source/conflict warnings, queue position and decision history.

**Responsive behavior:** desktop may compare source and draft side by side; narrow layouts stack them with source first. Keyboard shortcuts must not fire while typing.

**Exclude:** bulk blind approval, learner-facing preview without approved data, course reordering and hidden AI provenance.
